Questions

Will my current clinician be offended?

Most are not. Second opinions are routine in every other specialty.

Are you trying to take me on as a patient?

No. Most people go back to their own clinician, better equipped.

Will you change my medication?

No. Recommendations go to you and, with your consent, to your treating clinician, who makes the decisions.

Can you guarantee you will find something?

No. Sometimes the original diagnosis holds. What I guarantee is a thorough look and a clear written answer.

Why don't you take insurance?

With no insurance claim, no diagnosis code goes to an insurer, and no insurer decides how many visits you get. A clinical record still exists, it can be subpoenaed, and mandatory reporting rules still apply. A superbill is available if you want to seek out-of-network reimbursement yourself, which does share a diagnosis code with your insurer.
