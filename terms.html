Everything on this site is general education. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and reading it or contacting me does not create a clinician-patient relationship. Talk to your own clinician about your situation.

Not Currently in Practice

I am a registered nurse (BSN, RN) completing graduate training as a psychiatric-mental health nurse practitioner. I am not a licensed advanced practice registered nurse, I am not accepting patients, and I do not provide psychiatric evaluation, treatment, or prescribing.

Planned Services Are a Preview

Pages describing services and fees show what I plan to offer once I am licensed and the practice opens in 2028. They are not offers, nothing can be booked or bought now, and final services and fees may change, including for inflation. Care will be available only to people physically in Illinois at each visit.

Founding Rates

Founding rates are offered when the practice opens, in sign-up order, to people on the list who are in Illinois. They are locked for the first year and then move to the standard rate. Joining the list does not guarantee a place.
