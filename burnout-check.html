Is It Burnout, or Just a Busy Season?

Seven questions from the work-related scale of the Copenhagen Burnout Inventory, a free and widely used research measure. Your answers stay in your browser. Nothing is sent or saved.

1Is your work emotionally exhausting?
2Do you feel burned out because of your work?
3Does your work frustrate you?
4Do you feel worn out at the end of the working day?
5Are you exhausted in the morning at the thought of another day at work?
6Do you feel that every working hour is tiring for you?

    Scale: Kristensen TS, Borritz M, Villadsen E, Christensen KB. The Copenhagen Burnout Inventory: a new tool for the assessment of burnout. Work & Stress, 2005. Work-related burnout scale; average of seven items, 0 to 100. Scores of 50 or more are commonly treated as high burnout.
