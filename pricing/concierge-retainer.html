Who this is for

Most people do not need this, and I will say so directly if you do not. Standard evaluation and follow-up visits serve the large majority of patients well.

The retainer exists for a narrower situation: someone whose schedule genuinely cannot accommodate waiting three weeks for an appointment, who needs to reach a clinician the same day when something changes, and who is managing psychiatric care alongside other physicians who need to be coordinated with. Founders mid-raise, physicians between call blocks, litigators in trial, people who travel constantly.

What is included

  • Direct access. My phone number, not an answering service. Calls and messages answered the same day during business hours.
  • Priority scheduling. Appointments within 48 hours when something changes. Standard patients wait weeks; you do not.
  • Extended visits. Appointments scheduled at 60 minutes rather than 30, at no additional charge regardless of frequency.
  • Unlimited visits. Come as often as is clinically useful. No per-visit charge and no counting.
  • Care coordination. Direct communication with your primary care physician, therapist, and other specialists, so you are not the one carrying messages between clinicians.
  • Travel continuity. Telehealth continuity anywhere I am licensed, and prescription coordination while you are away.
  • Annual comprehensive review. A full reassessment each year rather than incremental adjustments.

Why it costs what it costs

Same-day access is only possible if the schedule is not full. A retainer patient occupies capacity that would otherwise hold several standard patients, whether or not they use it in a given week. That reserved capacity is the entire product, and it is what the fee pays for.

This is also why the panel is deliberately small. Beyond a certain number of retainer patients the promise stops being deliverable, and a promise that cannot be kept is worse than no promise at all.

What is not included

Emergency and crisis services. A retainer is not a substitute for emergency care. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 or go to your nearest emergency department. Hospitalization, intensive outpatient programs, and inpatient care are referred and billed separately by those providers.

The retainer also does not purchase a particular diagnosis, medication, or outcome. Clinical judgment is not for sale at any price.

Terms

  • Billed annually in advance. Quarterly payment available.
  • Twelve-month term, renewable.
  • Cash-pay. No insurance billing. A superbill is provided for your own out-of-network submission if you wish.
  • If the fit is wrong within the first 60 days, the unused portion is refunded.

Most people should start here instead

If you are not certain this is right for you, it probably is not. A standard initial evaluation at $250 provides the same clinical assessment, the same diagnostic thinking, and the same treatment planning. Follow-up visits at $125 handle ongoing care well for nearly everyone.

The retainer buys access and time. It does not buy better medicine.
