Is Worry Running the Show?

Seven questions from the GAD-7, the anxiety questionnaire used in most doctors’ offices. Think about the last two weeks. Your answers stay in your browser. Nothing is sent or saved.

Over the last 2 weeks, how often have you been bothered by:

1Feeling nervous, anxious, or on edge
2Not being able to stop or control worrying
3Worrying too much about different things
4Trouble relaxing
5Being so restless that it is hard to sit still
6Becoming easily annoyed or irritable
