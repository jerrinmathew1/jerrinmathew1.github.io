Questions

Is $400 a lot for one appointment?

It is a 60–90 minute assessment plus a written plan. Shorter first visits often need several appointments to cover the same ground.

Will I be pressured to start medication?

No. The evaluation shows whether medication fits. The decision is yours.

I have already seen other providers.

Bring those records. They help fill in the picture and avoid repeating tests.

What happens afterward?

You get a written summary. Then you choose: follow-up visits, a therapy referral, behavioral changes first, or a mix.

Why don't you take insurance?

With no insurance claim, no diagnosis code goes to an insurer, and no insurer decides how many visits you get. A clinical record still exists, it can be subpoenaed, and mandatory reporting rules still apply. A superbill is available if you want to seek out-of-network reimbursement yourself, which does share a diagnosis code with your insurer.
