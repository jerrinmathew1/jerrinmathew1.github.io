Questions

Do I Need an Initial Evaluation First?

Yes. Membership is for ongoing care after a full evaluation, so the plan is built on a real diagnosis.

What If I Need More Than One Visit in a Month?

Extra visits are available at the standard follow-up fee, with priority scheduling.

Is Messaging for Emergencies?

No. Messages are answered within one business day. In a crisis, call or text 988 or go to an emergency department.

Can I Cancel?

Yes. After the first three months, membership is month to month.

How Is This Different From the Concierge Retainer?

The retainer adds direct phone access, appointments within 48 hours, and unlimited hour-long visits. Membership is steady monthly care with messaging, for people who do not need same-week access.

Why Don't You Take Insurance?

With no insurance claim, no diagnosis code goes to an insurer, and no insurer decides how many visits you get. A clinical record still exists, it can be subpoenaed, and mandatory reporting rules still apply. A superbill is available if you want to seek out-of-network reimbursement yourself, which does share a diagnosis code with your insurer.
