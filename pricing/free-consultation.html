Questions

I do not have time for another appointment.

It is 15 minutes by video. You will leave knowing whether a full evaluation is worth your time.

What if I do not need psychiatric care?

Then you will know, and that is a good outcome. Finding out is the point of the call.

Will this affect my career or license?

The consultation is not billed to insurance, so no diagnosis code goes to an insurer. Reporting rules differ by profession, so ask about yours on the call.

Is this a sales pitch for medication?

No. Medication is one option among several, and only when it fits.

Why don't you take insurance?

With no insurance claim, no diagnosis code goes to an insurer, and no insurer decides how many visits you get. A clinical record still exists, it can be subpoenaed, and mandatory reporting rules still apply. A superbill is available if you want to seek out-of-network reimbursement yourself, which does share a diagnosis code with your insurer.
