Mood & Depression

Depression and bipolar disorder do not always announce themselves. In high-functioning adults they more often look like irritability, overwork, or an unusually productive quarter.

What Low Mood Looks Like When You Keep Functioning

Depression in high performers rarely looks like the textbook picture. Work continues. Deadlines are met. Nobody at the office notices anything. What goes first is everything outside the job — the friendships, the interests, the sense that any of it means something.

It gets called burnout, because burnout is a more acceptable word and because the two look nearly identical from outside. The distinction matters, though, because one responds to rest and the other does not.

The writing here covers depression, bipolar spectrum presentations, and the specific problem of recognizing a mood disorder in someone whose output has not changed.
