How Founding Rates Work

I am the founder of this practice, and founding members are the first people to trust it: everyone who joined the waitlist before it opened. As a thank-you, they get a lower rate for their first year.

  1. Join the waitlist. It is free, and your place is set by when you sign up.
  2. When the practice opens in 2028, I offer founding rates in sign-up order. The first 25 who accept get the membership at $500 a month instead of $750. The first 10 who accept get the concierge retainer at $20,000 for the year instead of $30,000.
  3. If someone ahead of you passes, the offer moves to the next person on the list, so an early spot matters.
  4. Your founding rate is locked for 12 months, then moves to the standard rate.

Founding rates are for people in Illinois, where I will be licensed to practice.

How It Compares

Booked separately, a 45-minute follow-up ($300) and a 15-minute check-in ($125) come to $425. Membership is $750 a month ($500 for founding members in their first year) and adds a longer check-in, secure messaging, priority scheduling, and standing times. If you only need a check-in every two or three months, per-visit follow-ups are the better value.
