Questions

Do I Have to Keep Coming In?

Not forever. Check-ins matter most when starting a medication, after big life changes, or when symptoms shift.

Can I Ask Questions Between Visits?

Simple questions can go through the secure patient portal. Anything urgent gets a call or an earlier visit. In a crisis, call or text 988 or go to an emergency department.

When Is a 15-Minute Check-in Enough?

When your medication is working and nothing major has changed. If something comes up during a short visit, we can extend it or book a longer one.

Do You Charge for Forms and Letters?

Yes. A letter is $75, and longer forms such as FMLA or disability paperwork are $150.

What If I Want to Stop My Medication?

That is your choice. We plan a safe taper together, because stopping suddenly can cause problems.

What Is the Cancellation Policy?

Please give at least 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule. Missed appointments, and cancellations with less than 24 hours notice, are charged the full fee, because that time cannot be offered to someone else.

Can I Be Seen If I Am Outside Illinois?

No. I will be licensed only in Illinois, and Illinois is not part of the multistate agreement that lets nurse practitioners practice across state lines. You need to be physically in Illinois at every visit, in person or by telehealth. This applies to every service, including founding rates.

Why Don't You Take Insurance?

With no insurance claim, no diagnosis code goes to an insurer, and no insurer decides how many visits you get. A clinical record still exists, it can be subpoenaed, and mandatory reporting rules still apply. A superbill is available if you want to seek out-of-network reimbursement yourself, which does share a diagnosis code with your insurer.
