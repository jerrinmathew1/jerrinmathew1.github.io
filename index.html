Clinical Perspective

Why I Came to Psychiatry Through the ICU.

Critically ill patients teach you to notice small changes early, to think about brain and body as one system, and to stay methodical when a situation stops being simple.

My clinical career began in emergency medical services, then moved through ICU care, skilled nursing, cardiac telemetry, and neurocritical care at a Level I trauma center.

I am now completing graduate education in psychiatric-mental health nursing. The long-term goal is to bring that same discipline to outpatient psychiatric care for adults carrying significant professional responsibility.

Where I Actually Am

The Road to Prescribing, in Public.

Most practice sites appear the week someone starts taking patients. This one is being built in the open, well before it opens, so that when it does you can see exactly what stood behind it.

Right now: nights in a neuro ICU at a Level I trauma center in Chicago, and graduate school in between.

  1. MSN Nurse Practitioner Core

    Six courses — foundations, leadership, informatics, quality and safety, research methods, population health. Complete.

  2. Advanced Practice Core

    Advanced pathophysiology and advanced pharmacology fundamentals. Complete: 8 of 16 courses done.

  3. Psychiatric Specialty Coursework

    Psychopharmacology starting October 26, 2026, then advanced physical assessment and advanced psychiatric assessment in January and February 2027. Starting now.

  4. CCRN Certification

    The national critical care nursing certification, earned through bedside hours in the Cook County neuro ICU. Exam planned for early 2027.

  5. Clinical Practicum — 625 Supervised Hours

    Five practicum courses across differential diagnosis and management through the lifespan, taken one at a time, two months each, starting March 2027. The first, in March and April 2027, is with a psychiatrist (MD). 500 hours with a psychiatrist (MD), and 125 hours with a clinical psychologist (PsyD) learning psychotherapy.

  6. Graduation

    Master of Science in Nursing, Chamberlain University. Expected December 2027.

  7. Board Certification

    The ANCC examination for PMHNP-BC certification will be taken after graduation. 175 questions, three and a half hours, and everything downstream waits on it.

  8. Illinois Licensure

    APRN licensure will be applied for through IDFPR, and cannot be filed until board certification is in hand. Illinois controlled substance licensure and federal DEA registration will follow, strictly in that order.

  9. The Practice Opens

    Anticipated 2028. Everything on this site describes what is being built toward, not what is currently offered.

Approach

How I Intend to Practice.

  • Careful, unhurried assessment
  • Evidence-based treatment
  • Honest conversations about trade-offs
  • Medication as one tool among several
  • Coordination with the rest of your care
  • Long-term thinking about stability
  • Substance use assessed alongside mood, sleep, and attention
  • Attention to how you are doing, not only how you are performing
